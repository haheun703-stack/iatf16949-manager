The auditor questions what is the process in place to ensure stock rotation. The aude explains that when placing the product into stock, the newest product should be placed at the front and when picking the product, it should be picked from the rear. As this is a manual process prone to potential human error, the auditor should take a sample of different stored product and verify the process is effective. Stock rotation relates to IETF-16949 requirement 8.5.4.1 preservation supplemental which states the organization should use an infantry management system to optimize infantry turns over time and ensure stock rotation such as first in first out. It should be noted this this does not mandate first in first out but clearly states an organization must have an effective system for optimizing inference returns and ensuring stock rotation. Finally is observed the organization does not do receiving inspection only verification of the product matching the supplied paperwork and that the material certification checks have been completed by QC. This relates to IETF requirement 8.6.4 Four, verification and acceptance of conformity of externally provided products and services. The organization shall have a process to ensure the quality of externally provided processes, products and services utilizing one or more of the following methods. A receipt and evaluation of statistical data provided by the supplier to the organization. B receiving inspection and/or testing such as sampling based on performance. C. second party or third party assessments or audits of supplier sites when coupled with records of acceptable delivered product conformance to requirements. D part evaluation by a designated laboratory. E another method identified and agreed with the customer. This requirement gives an organization options to decide on the level of receiving inspection or verification based on risk. So let's summarize. In auditing any manufacturing process from material receipt to product dispatch, auditors should make reference to the manufacturing process flowchart, PFMEA and control plan related to the process being audited where defined as a requirement by the organization. Auditors should verify the relevant material certification and traceability records that these are received prior to product acceptance. When auditing product storage, it should be verified effective processes are in place for optimizing infantry turns over time and ensuring stock rotation, which could be a first in first out system.

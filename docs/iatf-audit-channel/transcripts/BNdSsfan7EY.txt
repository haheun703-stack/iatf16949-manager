Finally, the auditor seemed confused over the difference between customer requirements and customer specific requirements. The customer contract requirement stating minimum safety stock is a customer requirement, whereas customer specific requirements are interpretations or supplementary requirements linked with specific clause of this automotive QMS standard. In summary, to undertake an effective IATF 16949 audit, an auditor has to have a detailed understanding of the IATF 16949 requirements and also effective soft skills to effectively interface with auditees to collect objective evidence. Auditors should seek evidence of conformance and not just focus on finding nonconformities.

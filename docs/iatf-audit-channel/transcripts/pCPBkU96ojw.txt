We see in this video that the qualified auditor is accompanied by a trainee auditor. While this is a key activity in developing the competency of a new auditor, before the audit, it should be ensured that roles are clearly defined and that it's clear who will take the lead in asking the questions. A good practice adopted by the trainee auditors was to get the auditee to give an overview of the process, which then led to a review of the PFMEA. It was identified that the PFMEA in the AIAG reference manual format was last reviewed 15 months ago. The auditor was incorrect in saying this was not acceptable. The former FMEA will depend on customer-specific requirements. In this case, the auditee explained there was no CSRs, hence no requirement to transition to the AIAG PDA approach. However, the auditor should have followed the audit trail to question the auditee on how often the PFMEA and control plan are reviewed and if needed updated. The auditor questioned how the process is set up. The auditee stated that a first off is verified and demonstrates how this is done using a micrometer and a tape. This gives the opportunity to verify that the relevant controls are included in the control plan and follow the audit trail to verify how it is ensured that measuring equipment specified in the control plan is calibrated at the stated frequency and how measurement system variation MSA is understood.

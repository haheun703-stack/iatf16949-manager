In undertaking any an audit of any assembly process, it is important to verify the correct setup of the process with the correct tooling. Especially in this organization given the small batch quantities and the vast number of multiple product varants in an operator dependent assembly process. It's important to understand the identified risks and to verify how the risk have been controlled within the process as well as to verify records of a operator competency.

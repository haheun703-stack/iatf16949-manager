When undertaking audits in an organization with multiple sites certified under a corporate audit scheme, it is important auditors verify effective interactions between the site, the central location and any remote support locations. In this scenario, the auditor is auditing the process at the manufacturing site for setting and communicating the quality objectives. The audity explains that the corporate head office set the high level objectives for each site and communicates these annually. This relates to ISO 90001 requirement 6.2.1 and 6.2.2 and IETF requirement which states 6.2.2.1 2.1 quality objectives and planning to achieve them. Supplemental top management shall ensure the quality objectives to meet customer requirements are defined, established and maintained for relevant functions, processes and levels throughout the organization. The results of the organization review regarding interested parties and their relevant requirements shall be considered when the organization establishes its annual at a minimum quality objectives and related performance targets which can be internal or external. The audity explains they are moving from a paperbased communication process to using electronic means and that the objectives are communicated and reviewed at daily meetings.

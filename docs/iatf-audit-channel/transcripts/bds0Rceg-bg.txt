In this case, the auditee explains that one of the special characteristics identified relates to line speed. A special process characteristic and the measuring system is verified as part of a regular maintenance check. Rather than following the audit trail, the auditor seemed to jump to a conclusion that there was a problem because the measuring system was not calibrated, but only verified. The auditor, before deciding on whether this could be a potential nonconformity, should have sought evidence on how the verification is undertaken and how traceability to a recognized national or international standard is achieved. IATF requirement 7.1.5.2 states, "The organization shall have a documented process for managing calibration verification records." There is no requirement mandating calibration for equipment used to measure special product or process characteristics. Regarding measuring equipment identification, IATF 7.1.5.2 measurement traceability states, "Note, a number or another identifier traceable to the device calibration records meets the intent of the requirement in ISO 9001:2015."

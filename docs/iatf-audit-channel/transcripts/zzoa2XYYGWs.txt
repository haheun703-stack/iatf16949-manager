It is defined in IETF-16949 requirement 9.2.2.3 manufacturing process audits that when undertaking audits, the following must be met. Within each individual audit plan, each manufacturing process should be audited on all shifts where it occurs, including the appropriate sampling of shift handover. There is also a similar requirement related to third-party audits that shift handovers are sampled for effectiveness during every audit.

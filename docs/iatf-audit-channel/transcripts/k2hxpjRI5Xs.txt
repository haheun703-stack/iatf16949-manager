One key competency of an IATF auditor is to demonstrate their ability to deal with conflicts. In the IATF audit guide, the skills include demonstrate calmness in the event of conflict, manage conflict with the client being audited, maintaining the integrity of the IATF 16949 scheme. Whilst the auditor is correct to state that in a third-party IATF audit, it is not acceptable for the consultant to be present, the way the auditor dealt with the conflict situation was not acceptable. The auditor seemed intent on finding nonconformities without adequately giving the client the opportunity to explain the process. Specifically, the process to control suspect product. The IATF requirement 8.7.1.3 states, "The organization shall ensure that a product with unidentified or suspect status is classified and controlled as a non-conforming product." The auditor jumped to the conclusion that the steel coil in question must be treated as a suspect product without sufficient evidence. He should have allowed the client to give a full explanation without constraints, interruptions, before deciding whether the situation represents a nonconformity. Audit trails could be followed back to check stock records, material traceability, and evidence of how in this case rusty product is processed. It is ensured at the end of the processing the material is fully compliant with the customer and all internal specifications.
